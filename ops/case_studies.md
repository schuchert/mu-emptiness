# Chapter 7: Worked Case Studies (Integration Tests)

### Case Study: The Cognitive Jam
**Scenario:** Staring at code, jaw clenching, reading the same line for 10 minutes.
1. **Diagnosis:** $\epsilon \gg 1$. Overfitting loop.
2. **Protocol:** Leave the terminal. Stand in Wu Chi (60s). Move to physical paper (Pen-to-Paper).
3. **Result:** Denominator rises ($RMSSD$). Cognitive rigidity breaks. Solution renders in the $11$ state.
