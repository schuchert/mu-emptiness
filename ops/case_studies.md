# Chapter 7: Worked Case Studies (Integration Tests)

## 🎯 1. Purpose
To provide concrete examples of the framework in action. These case studies serve as "Integration Tests," demonstrating how to map real-world friction ($t+$) to the internal Triadic Engine and verify the results via telemetry.

## 🔒 2. Canonical Definitions (Symbol Lock)
* **Isomorphic Mapping:** The act of treating a life event as a system error or state change.
* **The "Morning Jam":** A common 01 (Overfitting) state where Brett tries to solve the entire day's problems before Fred has finished booting.
* **The "Slack Attack":** A state of Social Friction where an external input triggers a rapid closing of the Sieve.

## 🛠️ 3. Operational Procedure (The "How")

### Case Study A: The 9 AM Cognitive Jam (State: 01)
1. **Scenario:** You sit at your desk. You have 4 meetings and a complex bug. Your chest feels tight. 
2. **The Audit:** * **Fred:** Signaling threat (Tight chest/Bruce is closed).
    * **Brett:** Looping on the "What if I fail?" narrative.
    * **Otto:** In reactive mode; checking email compulsively.
3. **The Protocol:** Execute **Chapter 1: The Shake**. Force a reset of the Vagal Brake. 
4. **The Result:** RMSSD rises from 35ms to 50ms. Brett is downgraded from "Panic" to "Planning."

### Case Study B: The Deep Work Sprint (State: 11)
1. **Scenario:** You are coding a complex script. You've lost track of time. You feel "The Animal" is calm but the mind is sharp.
2. **The Audit:**
    * **Fred:** Steady HR (85 BPM); breathing is deep and rhythmic.
    * **Brett:** Fully engaged in the logic-gate.
    * **Otto:** In "Flow" mode; external distractions are being filtered by the Sieve.
3. **The Protocol:** Do nothing. This is the **Full-Render** state. Maintain the Sieve.

### Case Study C: The "Bad Meh" (State: 10)
1. **Scenario:** After a high-conflict meeting, you feel "numb" or "checked out." You can't start the next task.
2. **The Audit:**
    * **Fred:** Low HR, but very low RMSSD. The body feels heavy.
    * **Brett:** Offline. Total apathy.
3. **The Protocol:** **IMMEDIATE EXIT.** Execute Chapter 1, Protocol B (5-Minute Shutdown). Do not try to "think" your way out.

## 📊 4. Telemetry Return Codes (The Data)
* **Case A (Success):** RMSSD delta of **+15%** post-shake.
* **Case B (Success):** RMSSD remains within **10%** of Morning Floor despite high cognitive load.
* **Case C (Success):** System returns to **00 (Baseline)** after 5 minutes of horizontal rendering.
