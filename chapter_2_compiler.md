# Chapter 2: The Kinesthetic Compiler

## 2.3 Baseline Telemetry: The IO Window
Do not rely on "vibes." Use Heart Rate (HR) and RMSSD (HRV) as your system return codes.

### The 5-Minute Baseline
Establish your $C_{base}$ daily. Sit in stillness for 5 minutes. Record your average RMSSD. If baseline is 50% below your rolling average, do not push into high-friction $t+$ tasks.

### The Coherence Signature
When engaging in work ($t+$):
1. **HR should RISE (5-10 BPM):** The engine is doing work.
2. **RMSSD should remain FLAT or RISE:** The Vagal Brake is holding. 

If HR spikes and RMSSD crashes, the Sieve has closed ($01$ state). Execute Shutdown.
