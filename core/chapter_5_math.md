## The Dynamic Planck-Ego Variable ($\epsilon$)
The $15\%$ Ego is a thermodynamic buffer managing the friction between the infinite Void ($t0$) and the biological Meat Unit.

### The Equation
$$\epsilon(t) = \frac{N(t)}{C_{base} + \alpha \cdot RMSSD(t)}$$

* **$N(t)$ [Noise]:** Real-time cognitive load (1-10 scale).
* **$C_{base}$ [Capacity]:** Structural hardware limit (Fixed 1-10).
* **$RMSSD(t)$ [Tone]:** Real-time HRV metric in milliseconds.

### The Three Regimes
1. **Overfitting ($\epsilon \gg 1$):** High noise, low HRV. Rigid control and anxiety. **Fix:** Raise the denominator (Shake).
2. **Optimal ($\epsilon \approx 1$):** Balanced Sieve. The $11$ Flow State. **Fix:** Maintain.
3. **Underfitting ($\epsilon \approx 0$):** Blown Sieve. Dissociation and "Bad Meh." **Fix:** Radical physical grounding.
